# Mac App Store previews

The owner approved the English cut on November 17, 2026 for Mac App Store publication. The Simplified Chinese cut is retained for review only.

| File | Intended use |
| --- | --- |
| [English preview](float-lyrics-mac-preview.mp4) | Approved for international submission. All explanatory captions are in English. |
| [Simplified Chinese preview](float-lyrics-mac-preview-zh-Hans.mp4) | Owner review only. Keep this version out of the international submission. App Store localization does not guarantee that media stays within a particular storefront. |

The two cuts share the same English app interface and Float Lyrics sample lines. The captions and playback-position label are localized. A suggested poster frame is **3.8 s**, when the large working document and translucent lyric window are both visible.

| Preview time | On screen |
| --- | --- |
| 0–4.8 s | The app’s native floating window shows live demo lyrics over the photo in a coastal field note. All writing remains visible in the left column. The Glass background is set to 32% opacity. |
| 4.8–19.0 s | A continuous playback demo. At 8.6 s, the playback position moves forward from about 0:05 to 0:27. At 9.6 s, the app shows “Follow along without switching apps.” At 16.0 s, the position moves back from about 0:34 to 0:08 and the current line returns to “Your music, in sync.” |
| 19.0–23.5 s | Compact and expanded native notch views. The edit switches to the expanded view at 21.2 s. |
| 23.5–27.5 s | A window-only excerpt from a macOS screen recording shows lyric color and the Window and Island selections in Appearance. |

For the seek demonstration, a local AVPlayer played an original 48-second recording. A capture-only adapter supplied measured playback positions to the app’s normal lyric selection and native floating view. The separate position bar plots the player’s measured time, and the ring appears during the two recorded seek gestures. The capture exercises the app’s lyric response in a controlled session. The live Music and Spotify integrations were outside the scope of this recording.

Each MP4 contains 825 progressive frames at 1920 × 1080 and 30 fps. The video uses H.264 High Profile Level 4.0, with a silent AAC-LC stereo track at 48 kHz and 256 kbps.
