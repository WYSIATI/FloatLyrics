# Float Lyrics support

## Connect your player

Open Spotify or Music on your Mac and play a song. In the Float Lyrics window, choose **••• → Music Player** and select the same player. Allow Automation when macOS asks. To change this later, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation**, then use **Settings → Music player → Reconnect** in Float Lyrics.

For a missing lyric, try **Settings → Music player → Refresh lyrics**. The service matches recordings by track details. A particular recording may have timed lyrics, plain text, or no match. If just the timing is off, adjust **Settings → Behavior & timing → Lyric timing** for that song.

## Find the lyric window

Click the Float Lyrics icon in the menu bar and choose **Show Window** or **Show Dynamic Island**. If mouse clicks go through the lyrics, open **Settings → Behavior & timing** from the menu bar and turn off **Click through lyrics**.

## Contact

For help or feedback, open an issue with your macOS version, Float Lyrics version and selected player. The issue tracker is public. Please keep private account details and listening history out of your report. [Contact the maintainers](https://github.com/WYSIATI/FloatLyrics/issues)

The privacy policy describes lyric lookups, local storage and how to change playback access. [Privacy policy](../../PRIVACY.md)
