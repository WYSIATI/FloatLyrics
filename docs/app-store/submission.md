## Set the price

In **Pricing and Availability → App Pricing**, choose the United States as the base country, use **See Additional Prices**, and look up the exact customer price **$999.00**. If available, select it, review the proposed prices for the other selected storefronts, save, and reopen the U.S. price schedule to verify it. If this amount is absent, leave pricing pending and ask the seller before selecting another price. Apple lists default U.S. price points through $1,000. Its additional pricing entitlement covers amounts above that level. The published U.S. matrix specifies $10 steps near $999 and lists supported endings without enumerating each combination. The available points for this app can also be checked by listing all U.S. price points in the authenticated App Store Connect API and matching the decimal customer price. [Apple pricing ranges](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/110364/) · [U.S. price matrix](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/pdfs/App-Store-Pricing-Update.pdf) · [List price points for an app](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appstoreconnectapi/get-v1-apps-_id_-apppricepoints)

## App Review notes

The following text can be entered in the **Notes** field after validating the signed Store build.

> Float Lyrics is a menu bar app for macOS. It displays lyrics for the song already playing in Spotify or Music on the same Mac. No Float Lyrics account is required.
>
> 1. Open Music or Spotify on this Mac and play a track. In Float Lyrics, open the floating window’s ••• menu, choose Music Player, and select that player. Allow the macOS Automation prompt.
> 2. The song title and artist appear in the floating window. For a recording with timed lyrics on LRCLIB, pause, resume, or seek in the player and the visible line follows. If only plain lyrics are available, the floating window labels them as unsynced.
> 3. Choose Move to Dynamic Island from •••, then click the line at the top of the display to expand it. A physical notch is optional. From the menu bar, open Settings → Appearance to adjust each view independently. The menu bar also restores hidden lyrics.
> 4. To inspect the interface with original sample text independently of player accounts or network availability, quit the app, then launch it from Terminal with `open -a "Float Lyrics" --args --demo --demo-position 15`. Quit and open the app normally to test the live player integration.
>
> Apple Events are used to read the selected player's current track and playback position so the overlay can synchronize. The app requests the player's scripting access groups for these read operations. It sends track title, artist and, when available, album and duration to LRCLIB over HTTPS. It caches lyric results locally. The app does not provide music streams, record audio, modify the music library or change playback.
