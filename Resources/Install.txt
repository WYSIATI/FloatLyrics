FLOATLYRICS
Your music, a little closer.

1. Drag FloatLyrics into Applications.
2. Open FloatLyrics. Look for the quotation bubble in your menu bar.
3. Choose Spotify or Apple Music, open that desktop app, and play a song.
4. Allow the macOS Automation prompt so FloatLyrics can read the track and position.

The floating window stays on top at 88% opacity. Use the sliders button to adjust
opacity, lyric size, timing, and click through. Drag the window to place it.

Use the notch button to tuck lyrics below the camera cutout. On a Mac without a
notch it becomes a compact strip below the menu bar. Expand it with the arrow.
The physical camera cutout itself cannot display text.

If click through is enabled or lyrics are hidden, use the menu bar to restore them.

Needs macOS 14 or later. Native Liquid Glass requires macOS 26.
Lyrics by LRCLIB. Some recordings have unsynced lyrics or no lyrics available.
Browser music players and playback on other devices are not supported.

Developer builds are ad-hoc signed unless a Developer ID and notarization profile
are supplied at build time. To open an unnotarized build you trust, use System
Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway after the initial launch attempt.
