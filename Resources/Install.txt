FLOATLYRICS
Your music, a little closer.

1. Drag FloatLyrics into Applications.
2. Open FloatLyrics. Look for the quotation bubble in your menu bar.
3. Choose Spotify or Apple Music, open that desktop app, and play a song.
4. Allow the macOS Automation prompt so FloatLyrics can read the track and position.

The floating window stays on top. Open Appearance with the sliders button,
the menu bar, or Command-comma. Window and Island have separate background
opacity and lyric colors. Choose Automatic, a color swatch, a custom color,
or enter a six-digit hex color. Changes preview live and are saved.
Choose Glass, Smoke or Ink for a neutral material. Appearance uses the same
glass panel and controls as the lyrics window. Text presets are automatic,
graphite, charcoal, silver and white; older blue/purple presets are replaced.
Lyrics stay crisp as the background fades. Drag the window to place it.

Use the MacBook button to enter Dynamic Island. Its black shape joins the camera
housing at the screen's top edge. It stays one line high and 320 points wide by
default. Click to expand; it automatically shrinks when you return to work.
Hover expansion is optional. Use the up chevron to collapse, or the window arrow
to return to floating lyrics. Adjust island width, background and text color in
the Island appearance tab. The camera cap stays black as the lyric area fades.

If click through is enabled or lyrics are hidden, use the menu bar to restore them.

Needs macOS 14 or later. Native Liquid Glass requires macOS 26.
Lyrics by LRCLIB. Some recordings have unsynced lyrics or no lyrics available.
Browser music players and playback on other devices are not supported.

Developer builds are ad-hoc signed unless a Developer ID and notarization profile
are supplied at build time. To open an unnotarized build you trust, use System
Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway after the initial launch attempt.
