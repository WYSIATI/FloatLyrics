FLOAT LYRICS
Your music, a little closer.

1. Drag Float Lyrics into Applications.
2. Open Float Lyrics. Look for the lifted-line F in your menu bar.
3. Choose Spotify or Apple Music, open that desktop app, and play a song.
4. Allow the macOS Automation prompt so Float Lyrics can read the track and position.

Updating from FloatLyrics: quit the old app and move FloatLyrics.app out of
Applications before installing Float Lyrics.app. Saved preferences are retained.

The floating window shows the song, current lyric and next line. Drag it to
place it. Open the three-dot menu for the music player, Dynamic Island,
Settings or Hide. The window stays on top by default.

Settings opens with three choices: Music player, Appearance, Behavior & timing.
Appearance holds separate Window and Island opacity and neutral text colors.
More options holds custom colors, hex entry, material, text size and island layout.
Changes apply immediately and are saved. Open Settings with Command-comma or
from the menu bar. Lyrics stay crisp as the background fades.

Use the three-dot menu to enter Dynamic Island. Its black shape joins the camera
housing at the screen's top edge. It stays one line high and 320 points wide by
default. Click to expand; it automatically shrinks when you return to work.
Hover expansion is optional. Use the up chevron to collapse, or the window arrow
to return to floating lyrics. Adjust island background and text color under
Settings > Appearance > Island, and width under More options. The camera cap
stays black as the lyric area fades.

If click through is enabled or lyrics are hidden, use the menu bar to restore them.

Needs macOS 14 or later. Native Liquid Glass requires macOS 26.
Lyrics by LRCLIB. Some recordings have unsynced lyrics or no lyrics available.
Browser music players and playback on other devices are not supported.

Compare the release's SHA-256 checksum before installing. Source is available
under the MIT license at https://github.com/WYSIATI/FloatLyrics.
